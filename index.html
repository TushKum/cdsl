The gap

Broker apps stop at the Buy button

A new investor sees a price, a chart and a green number. Nobody shows them where the shares actually live, who protects them, or what to do when a stranger on WhatsApp asks for an OTP. That missing layer is the depository, and it is where trust is built.

What a typical app shows

  • ·A price and a Buy button
  • ·A chart that goes up and down
  • ·Profit and loss in green and red
  • ·Tips, streaks and “trending stocks”

What Pehla Kadam adds

  • ✓Why shares arrive the next day (T+1), and the CDSL SMS that confirms it
  • ✓Your TPIN as the lock on every sale
  • ✓A monthly CAS statement you learn to check
  • ✓Dividends, bonus shares and splits as they happen
  • ✓Pledge, nominee and e-voting, with the risks explained
  • ✓A game that trains you to spot investment scams
How it works

Follow 20 shares of ITC through one simulated month

The prototype runs on a simulated calendar. Tap “Next day” and the depository does its work. This is exactly what a learner sees.

  1. T · Mon 1 Jun

    Buy

    Money leaves the trading account today. The shares show as “pending”.

  2. T+1 · Tue 2 Jun

    Credited to demat

    The depository moves the shares into the learner’s own name.

    CDSL (simulated)20 shares of ITC credited to your BO ID ending 4417.
  3. Record date · Fri 5 Jun

    Dividend

    ₹6.50 × 20 shares = ₹130 lands in the account, with a lesson on record dates.

  4. Ex-date · Thu 11 Jun

    Bonus 1:1

    Infosys holders get one free share per share. The price halves and the value stays the same.

  5. Ex-date · Wed 17 Jun

    Split 1 → 5

    Asian Paints: five times the shares at a fifth of the price. Like breaking a ₹500 note.

  6. Month end · Wed 1 Jul

    CAS arrives

    The monthly statement lists every movement. The learner checks it against their own records.

What you learn

Eight flows. Each one works, and each one teaches.

01

Open a demat account

Demo KYC, choose a broker, get a 16-digit BO ID and a TPIN.

Learn: trading account vs demat account, broker vs depository.
02

Buy and sell

Market orders, pending shares, a CDSL SMS on T+1, TPIN to sell.

Learn: settlement, eDIS, why depository alerts protect you.
03

Holdings and CAS

Portfolio with average price and gain/loss, plus a monthly statement.

Learn: what a CAS is and the four things to check.
04

Corporate actions

A dividend, a bonus and a split on fixed days, with a calendar.

Learn: record dates and why value stays the same.
05

Pledge

Lock shares for margin with a CDSL OTP. Watch margin fall when prices fall.

Learn: haircuts, margin calls, invocation risk.
06

Nominee

Add a nominee in a minute, or see what your family faces without one.

Learn: nomination vs legal-heir paperwork.
07

E-voting

An AGM resolution in three plain lines. Vote For, Against or Abstain.

Learn: one share, one vote: you are an owner.
08

Scam Shield

Five WhatsApp messages. Mark each one Safe or Scam.

Learn: red flags and how to verify SEBI registration.

Who it’s for

First-time investors in tier-2 and tier-3 towns, especially women

Many families hold shares that one person manages and nobody else understands. Pehla Kadam is built so that a woman in Indore, Nashik or Gorakhpur can practise safely, in her own language, before any real rupee is at stake.
